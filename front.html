TOP STORY — DAY TWO
HUANG'S SECOND ACT
SHOCKS SILICON VALLEY
Nvidia's board brought its founder back without telling its own managers — and Donald Trump phoned into the meeting. Shares soared as much as 37% in a day, the biggest jump since 2000.
▸ The reinstatement was decided Sunday, announced Monday — but the how only emerged today: interim chief Colette Kress and top executives learned of it last-minute.
▸ Washington blacklisted Megaspeed, the Singapore firm at the centre of the Nvidia spy scandal, from US technology.
▸ The board now says the return poses no national-security risk; its own investigation is still running.
SOURCES: BLOOMBERG, 24 NOV 07:28 UTC • AP • US BUREAU OF INDUSTRY AND SECURITY
ZELENSKY: FOUR-WAY TALKS, NOW
Ukraine's president calls for Kyiv, Moscow, Washington and Europe to meet as soon as possible — "ready as early as Tuesday". Kyiv also says its drones hit a Russian warship at a Libyan naval base, and leaked data put Russia's war dead near 700,000.
SOURCES: AP • KYIV INDEPENDENT
NATO: WE WILL DEFEND SPACE
The alliance vows to defend against threats in orbit after reports that Russia is preparing a nuclear-armed satellite, possibly launching as soon as December. Space, NATO says, is now a domain to defend like land and sea.
